Arrow Escape - Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026
Developer: AIFlow Systems (lsszz210@kakao.com)
Contact: leemanrank@gmail.com

Arrow Escape ("the app") is a local-first puzzle game. This policy explains what data is and is not processed when you use it.

Data stored on your device

Game progress (stage, removed tiles, hint count), language and sound settings are stored only on your device using standard app preferences. Android backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled. Uninstalling the app deletes this local data. No account, sign-in or game server is used.

Advertising and consent (Google)

The app shows ads through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) and uses Google's consent management (UMP) where required. Google and its partners may process:

  • device and advertising identifiers,
  • approximate location derived from IP address,
  • ad interactions and diagnostics,

for the purposes of providing ads, fraud prevention, and consent management, under Google's own privacy policies:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Where required (for example in the EEA/UK), a consent form is shown and a "Privacy choices" entry is available in the app's settings so consent can be reviewed or changed at any time. Ads never block gameplay: every stage can be cleared without watching an ad, and rewarded ads run only when you tap the reward button.

Data sharing and sale

The developer does not collect, sell or share personal data. Data processed by Google under its own policies is not controlled by the developer; uninstalling the app does not erase data already held by Google. Requests about that data should follow Google's own tools (for example My Ad Center).

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle and is not directed at children under 13. Ad content rating is set to G.

Data deletion

All developer-controlled data is on-device and is deleted by uninstalling the app. For questions or deletion requests, contact leemanrank@gmail.com.

Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.

Disclaimer

The app is provided for entertainment only. No medical, therapeutic or wellness outcome is promised.
