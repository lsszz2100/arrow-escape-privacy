Spireviella Privacy Policy

Effective November 25,2026. Developer: 이상선. Contact: lsszz2100@gmail.com.

Spireviella is an idle single-tower defense game. It has no game account, game server, chat or user-created public content.

Local data

Expedition progress, medals, workshop coins, research, tower appearance, last-active time, language, sound settings and tutorial state stay in this app's device storage. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Uninstalling removes the local game data controlled by the developer. It does not remove records held by Google.

Ads and consent

Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) provides banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google's User Messaging Platform manages consent where needed. These SDKs may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for ads, analytics, security, fraud prevention and consent. SDK network traffic is encrypted in transit. We request ads only after the consent check allows initialization. Settings has Ad privacy choices for regions where Google makes this available.

Google privacy information: Privacy policy and Advertising. Google provides privacy controls and deletion tools for information it controls. The developer does not sell separately held personal data.

Optional purchase

The free game offers one optional nonconsumable purchase to remove forced banner and interstitial ads. Optional rewarded videos remain optional. The explanation dialog displays Google's current localized price before opening the Play purchase screen. Google Play handles the payment, purchase history and refund rules. The developer does not receive your card or bank details. The app reads the purchase product, state and token to acknowledge ownership and restore it using the same Google Play account. Purchase entitlement is cached locally for offline use and checked with Google when available. Uninstalling does not cancel a completed nonconsumable purchase. Restore purchases from settings.

Permissions and audience

Internet and network-state access support ads, consent and billing. The advertising ID permission supports AdMob. No camera, microphone, contact list, precise GPS or photo-library access is requested. This is a general-audience game, not designed specifically for children. Ads use Google's maximum content rating G.

Offline play and disclaimer

Core gameplay works offline. Ads, new purchase/restore checks and consent updates need a network. Gold and scores have no cash value. There is no gambling, cash redemption or real-world prize. This game is entertainment and promises no financial or health benefit.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
