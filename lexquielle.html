Lexquielle Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026
Developer: 이상선

Lexquielle is an offline English word-tiles puzzle game with optional Korean clues and interface with no game account, login, game server, chat or user-generated content.

Local game data

Unlocked puzzles, language, tutorial and sound settings are saved only in app storage on your device. Android cloud backup and device transfer are disabled. Uninstall to remove developer-controlled local game data. Uninstalling does not erase data already held by third-party advertising services.

Advertising and consent

The app contains banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), with Google's User Messaging Platform for consent where required. These services may collect and share approximate location derived from IP address, advertising/device identifiers, app interactions and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, security, fraud prevention and consent. Data is encrypted in transit by the SDK. Ads are requested only when Google's consent status permits requests. Where required, you can revisit Privacy choices in settings. No personalized advertising is promised when consent is unavailable.

Google's policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads . Google provides its own privacy and deletion tools for data it controls. Contact the developer for questions about this app. We do not sell developer-held personal information; the app's ad SDK sharing is disclosed above.

Permissions and children

No camera, microphone, contacts, precise GPS location or photo-library access is requested. Internet and network state support ads and consent; the Android advertising ID permission supports the advertising SDK. This is a general-audience game, not specifically designed for children. Ad content is configured at Google's G maximum content rating. Store target-audience selections and ad settings must match the actual audience and applicable law.

Offline use and rewards

Core gameplay works offline. Ads, consent updates and ad rewards require network availability. Scores have no monetary value. There are no bets, purchases, transfers, redemptions, cashouts or real-world prizes. No real-money or therapeutic outcome is promised. The app is for entertainment.

Changes

Policy changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.
